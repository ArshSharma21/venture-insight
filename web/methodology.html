How this data is used

Venture Insight combines a dated large-company financial snapshot, historical Indian startup funding, and supplemental community-maintained company profiles. Every metric stays with its source; startup fundraising is never presented as operating revenue or profit.

Global public companies

Forbes Global 2000 Companies 2025 on Kaggle, listed CC0 Public Domain. It provides 2,000 ranked public companies, headquarters country, industry, sales, profit, assets and market value. Financial values are in USD billions and reflect the latest 12 months available to Forbes as of 25 April 2025. The CSV file is semicolon-delimited.

The dashboard calculates profit margin as reported profit divided by reported sales. This snapshot alone does not provide multi-year financial statements, liabilities, or business descriptions. Companies outside the list, including most private firms, are not represented in this financial source.

Supplemental company profiles and dated financial claims

Wikidata Query Service contributes 1,275 exact-English-name candidate profiles matched against the 2,000 Forbes names. Available fields include descriptions, websites, founding dates, headquarters, industries, employee facts, tickers, and exchanges. Matching exact names can select the wrong entity, so the modal links every candidate to its Wikidata page for review.

The selected profiles yielded 8,578 dated revenue, net-profit, and total-assets claims across 417 companies. Values retain the units/currencies and dates returned by Wikidata and are never added to or blended with Forbes figures. They are community-maintained claims, not audited facts. Claims with unclear units or reporting dates are not displayed. No total-liabilities claims were found in this retrieval.

Indian startup funding

Indian Startup Funding on Kaggle, listed CC0 Public Domain. The downloaded file has 3,044 records, of which 3,036 dates parse successfully between 2 January 2015 and 13 January 2020. Fields include company name, sector, sub-sector, city, investors, financing type, amount, and remarks.

A record describes a reported financing event. Missing or undisclosed amounts are not estimated. Repeated startup names may represent separate rounds, not separate companies. The dataset is historical and does not establish present-day status or performance.

Idea classification and recommendations

The idea explorer uses readable keyword rules to suggest sector labels and compares idea words with company names and available profile, category, sub-sector, and remarks fields. A signal percentage reflects only the rule matches, not calibrated model confidence. Match scores are text overlap, not proof of direct competition or market opportunity. Review the matched terms and source fields before drawing conclusions.

Coverage is broader but still incomplete: financial snapshots cover 2,000 large listed companies, Wikidata profiles are uneven, and the Indian startup funding source ends in 2020. The SEC filing API was researched, but requests from this environment were denied, so no SEC facts were included. The platform does not provide investment, legal, or commercial-success predictions.

Dataset documentation and reproducible raw files are in data/README.md.
